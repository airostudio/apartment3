Stripe Connect Setup Guide

One-time setup
Two Stripe accounts are required: the Admin Platform account (with Connect enabled) and the Owner's connected account (typhoon.tall69@gmail.com). This is a static demo — a server-side backend is required for real transactions.

Step 1 — Admin Platform Account

  1. Create a Stripe account at stripe.com for the admin/management business and complete business verification.
  2. Go to Settings → Connect settings and enable Stripe Connect. Choose Express accounts for the owner onboarding.
  3. Copy your Publishable Key (pk_live_...) and Connect Client ID (ca_...) into js/stripe-connect.js.
  4. Add your bank account in Stripe Dashboard → Settings → Bank Accounts to receive admin management fees.

Step 2 — Connect Owner Account (typhoon.tall69@gmail.com)

  1. On your server, call stripe.accounts.create{type: 'express'} to create an Express connected account for the owner.
  2. Generate an Account Link and send it to typhoon.tall69@gmail.com so they can complete Stripe's onboarding (identity + bank details).
  3. Once completed, store the owner's acct_... account ID in your database.

Step 3 — Process Payments with Destination Charges

  1. On your server, create PaymentIntents using Destination Charges: 
    stripe.paymentIntents.create({
  amount: 100000,             // $1,000.00 in cents
  currency: 'aud',
  application_fee_amount: 15000, // 15% admin fee = $150
  transfer_data: {
    destination: 'acct_conn_9ZyXwVuTsRq', // owner's acct_id
  },
});
  2. Stripe automatically transfers $850.00 to the owner's account and $150.00 (minus Stripe fees) stays in the platform account.
  3. Set up webhook endpoints for payment_intent.succeeded and transfer.created to update booking records.
