Your Alpine Base at Mt Baw Baw

Cascade Apartment 3 is a recently renovated, fully self-contained apartment set over two levels in the heart of Baw Baw Village. Nestled among snowgums with direct ski-in/ski-out access to the slopes, it's the perfect base for all seasons.

The large open-plan living area features three couches, a flat-screen TV, and a gas log fire to warm up après-ski. The fully equipped kitchen has everything you need, including a microwave and air fryer. Hydronic heating throughout keeps the whole apartment cosy, with a dedicated drying room for your gear.

Bedroom 1 has a comfortable Queen bed. Bedroom 2 features two tri-bunks — sleeping up to 6 in bunk configurations — making it ideal for families and groups. Step onto the private balcony for sweeping mountain views, with resort parking close by.
