What to Bring

Everything you need to know before you pack for your alpine getaway at Cascade Apartment 3.

Please bring your own linen & towels

Cascade Apartment 3 is a linen-not-included property. Guests are required to bring their own bed linen (fitted sheets, pillowcases, and top sheets) and towels for each person in your group. Doonas, blankets, and pillows are provided for every bed.

You Need to Bring
Pack these for your stay
  • Bed linen — fitted sheets, top sheets, and pillowcases for each bed you'll use
  • Bath towels — one per person
  • Hand towels & face washers
  • Personal toiletries — shampoo, conditioner, soap, etc.
  • Food & groceries — the nearest supermarket is in Rawson (approx. 20 min)
  • Warm clothing & layers — alpine temperatures can drop significantly, even in summer
  • Snow chains — mandatory when conditions apply (ski season)
Already Provided
Ready and waiting for you
  • Doonas / duvets — for every bed
  • Blankets — extra blankets available in wardrobes
  • Pillows — one per person per bed
  • Wood fireplace — firewood provided during ski season
  • Fully equipped kitchen — cookware, utensils, plates, glasses & cutlery
  • Washing machine & dryer
  • Wi-Fi, Smart TV & streaming

Handy Tips for Your Trip

Check-in from 2:00 PM Checkout by 11:00 AM. Early check-in / late checkout available as an add-on.
Nearest shops in Rawson Stock up before heading up the mountain — approx. 20 min drive away.
Alpine weather Pack layers regardless of season — temperatures can drop quickly at altitude.
Snow chains in winter Carry chains during ski season — they are mandatory when the road conditions require them.
