About the Apartment

Solid brick comfort
at 1,478 m above sea level

Cascade Apartment 6 is a fully self-contained, solid-brick apartment set over two levels in the heart of Baw Baw Village. Recently renovated with high-quality finishes, it comfortably sleeps up to 10 guests across three bedrooms — perfect for families, ski groups or a special long weekend.

The open-plan living area features a flat screen TV, a gas heater for those crisp mountain evenings, and a balcony with sweeping alpine views. Downstairs is warmed by hydronic heating throughout. A dedicated drying room means wet ski gear is never a problem.

A Perfect Retreat, Every Season

Mt Baw Baw comes alive in both winter and summer

⛷

Winter Season

Victoria's closest alpine resort to Melbourne transforms into a snow-covered wonderland. Ski and snowboard runs for all abilities, tobogganing, and snowshoeing — all within minutes of your door.

Downhill Skiing Snowboarding Cross-country Tobogganing Snowshoeing
🌿

Summer Season

When the snow melts, Mt Baw Baw reveals lush alpine meadows, ancient snow gum forests, and world-class trails. Enjoy cool mountain air while the city swelters below.

Mountain Biking Bushwalking Fishing Bird Watching Photography
