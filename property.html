Cascade Apartment 3

Baw Baw Village, Mt Baw Baw, VIC 3833 4.8 (47 reviews) Self-Contained Apartment

Cascade Apartment 3 is a fully self-contained, solid-brick apartment set over two levels right in the heart of Baw Baw Village. Recently renovated with high-quality finishes, it comfortably sleeps up to 10 guests — making it ideal for large families, ski groups, school holiday escapes, or a memorable long-weekend gathering.

The bright, open-plan living area features three comfortable couches, a flat screen TV, a gas heater for those crisp mountain evenings, and a balcony with spectacular alpine views. The fully equipped kitchen includes a full-size fridge/freezer, dishwasher, oven, microwave, and all the cookware you need for a complete stay.

Downstairs is warmed by hydronic heating throughout, and a dedicated drying room means wet ski gear and jackets can be sorted out without cluttering the living space. With Mt Baw Baw Village just steps from your door, you're minutes from the snow in winter and the trailheads in summer.
