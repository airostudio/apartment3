Cascade Apartment 3

Baw Baw Village, Mt Baw Baw, VIC 3833 (28 reviews) Apartment

Welcome to Cascade Apartment 3, a recently renovated, fully self-contained apartment set over two levels in the heart of Baw Baw Village at Mt Baw Baw. Nestled among snowgum trees with direct ski-in/ski-out access, this alpine retreat is the perfect base for all seasons — from winter skiing to summer hiking and mountain biking.

The large open-plan living and dining area features three couches, a flat-screen TV, CD player, and a gas log fire to keep you warm après-ski. The fully equipped kitchen includes a fridge/freezer, stove, oven, microwave, dishwasher, sandwich maker, and air fryer. Hydronic heating runs throughout the apartment, and a dedicated drying room means you're always ready for the next day on the mountain.

Bedroom 1 (downstairs) has a comfortable Queen bed. Bedroom 2 (downstairs) features two tri-bunks — each with a double on the bottom and a single on top — sleeping up to 6 in this room alone. Combined, the apartment sleeps up to 8 guests across two bathrooms. Step out onto the private balcony for sweeping alpine views, with resort parking just a short walk away.
